# Awesome TikTok Ad Library

*Unofficial community list for the TikTok Ad Library. Not affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance. All trademarks belong to their owners.*

A curated list of tiktok ad library pages and the tools around them: the Commercial Content Library for what has run, Creative Center Top Ads for what performed, the policy pages you need before copying a format, and the creator and creative-production surfaces that sit next to them. Every link is a page these tools publish themselves.

## Official resources

- [Commercial Content Library - Ad Library](https://library.tiktok.com/ads) - TikTok's public record of ads; the page most people mean by ad library.
- [All ads report](https://library.tiktok.com/ads/report) - the aggregate report view inside the library.
- [Other commercial content](https://library.tiktok.com/other-commercial-content) - the non-ad commercial content section of the library.
- [Commercial Content Library FAQs](https://library.tiktok.com/faq) - what the library includes and how to read it; read once before searching.

## Getting started

- [Creative Center Top Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/topads) - high-performing auction ads with Objective, Region, Sort by, Likes and Duration filters.
- [Creative Center Top Ads (English)](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/topads/pc/en) - the English desktop entry to the same dashboard.
- [TikTok Creative Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/creative/creativeCenter) - home for Top Ads, Trends and Creative Tools.
- [Creative Center Guidelines](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article?aid=14099) - the rules for using Creative Center content.

## Tutorials and articles

- [Ad Policies](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article?aid=10000962) - what is allowed before you reproduce a format you found.
- [Ad Specifications](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article?aid=10002742) - formats, sizes and lengths that will actually be accepted.
- [TikTok for Business Help Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/) - articles on the advertising tools.

## Tools and integrations

- [Creative Center Trends](https://ads.tiktok.com/creative/creativeCenter/trends) - what is trending, next to the ads that use it.
- [Symphony Creative Studio](https://ads.tiktok.com/creative/creativeCenter/tools/creativeTools) - text to video, image to video, avatar videos, product remixes, translation and dubbing into 15 languages.
- [Creative Center Automation](https://ads.tiktok.com/creative/creativeCenter/tools/automation) - automation tools under Creative Tools.
- [Creative Center API](https://ads.tiktok.com/creative/creativeCenter/tools/api) - the API entry under Creative Tools; check scope and terms there.
- [TikTok Ads Manager](https://ads.tiktok.com/i18n/home) - where the campaigns you benchmark against are actually run.
- [Creative Exchange](https://ads.tiktok.com/creativeexchange) - listed under Products and Services in the Creative Center footer.
- [TikTok for Developers](https://developers.tiktok.com/) - the developer portal if you are looking for programmatic access.
